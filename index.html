Frequently Asked Questions

Do you deliver to all parts of Accra?

Yes, we deliver to all areas within Accra and surrounding regions. Contact us for delivery details and rates based on your location.

What is the minimum order quantity?

We serve both retail and wholesale customers. There is no minimum order quantity for retail customers. For bulk wholesale orders, please contact us for special pricing.

Are your cables certified?

Yes, all our cables meet international quality standards and safety certifications. We only supply certified products that ensure optimal performance and safety.

Absolutely! Our team of experts provides professional consultation to help you choose the right cable for your specific project requirements. Contact us anytime for advice.
